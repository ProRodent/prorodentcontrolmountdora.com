Information we collect.

When you call or email us, we collect the information you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, address and a description of the rodent problem. This information is used to schedule and carry out a visit, and to follow up on the service afterward.

How we use it.

We use the information you provide to route your call, plan a technician's visit, and keep a record of the service performed at your address. We do not sell your information to third parties, and we do not use it for marketing unrelated to rodent control service.

No online forms.

This site does not use a contact form or online booking system that collects visitor information automatically. All contact happens by phone or email, which you initiate directly.

Website analytics.

Like most websites, this site may use basic analytics to understand which pages are visited, without identifying individual visitors personally. This data helps us keep the site useful and up to date.

Contacting us about your information.

If you have questions about information you have shared with us, or want it removed from our records, email contact@prorodentcontrolmountdora.com or call (352) 358-2461.
