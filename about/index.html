Why we describe locations honestly.

A community's actual government status matters more than it might seem when you are trying to find local help. Calling an unincorporated community like Zellwood or Sorrento a town with its own city government would be inaccurate, and we would rather describe a place correctly than borrow language that sounds more official than the community's real structure. This same standard applies across our areas page and every individual location page it links to.

What a typical visit looks like.

A first visit generally starts with a conversation on the phone about what has been noticed: sounds, droppings, damage, or a direct sighting. From there, a technician schedules an on-site inspection to confirm the species involved and locate the entry point or points responsible. Depending on what that inspection finds, the visit may include trapping and removal, sealing work, attic or crawl space cleanup, or repair of chewed material, scoped and explained before any work beyond the inspection itself begins.
