The local government picture in Grand Island.

Grand Island is a small unincorporated community in Lake County situated on a peninsula between Lake Yale and Lake Eustis, a quiet, low-density area of citrus and residential lots.

No mayor, city hall or standalone police force serves Grand Island, since the community is unincorporated. Lake County handles the local government functions a chartered town would otherwise provide. That has no effect on how we cover the area, since coverage tracks ZIP code rather than local government structure.

No walk-in office sits in Grand Island itself. Our home base is in Mount Dora, and once a call comes in from a Grand Island ZIP we route a technician out to handle it directly, the same as we would for any other address on our coverage list.

Homes on Grand Island's peninsula sit close to water on more than one side, and that proximity to two lakes brings the same natural rodent habitat pressure found around Mount Dora's own lakefront.
