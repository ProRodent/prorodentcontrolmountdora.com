The local government picture in Plymouth.

Plymouth is a small unincorporated community in Orange County northwest of Apopka, a quiet crossroads area of citrus groves and low-density residential lots rather than a dense town center.

Being unincorporated, Plymouth has no city government of its own to speak of, no mayor, no separate city hall. Orange County fills that role instead, providing the local services a town charter would otherwise cover. We still route calls here purely by ZIP code, independent of that distinction.

You will not find a storefront for us inside Plymouth. A Plymouth ZIP call routes from Mount Dora to a technician headed to your address, the same setup used for the rest of our coverage area.
