The local government picture in Sorrento.

Sorrento is a small unincorporated community in Lake County northeast of Mount Dora, a rural crossroads area known locally for its blueberry farms and low-density residential streets.

Being unincorporated, Sorrento has no city government of its own to speak of, no mayor, no separate city hall. Lake County fills that role instead, providing the local services a town charter would otherwise cover. We still route calls here purely by ZIP code, independent of that distinction.

There is no storefront to visit in Sorrento. A call from a Sorrento ZIP gets routed from Mount Dora straight to a technician, who comes to you rather than the other way around.
